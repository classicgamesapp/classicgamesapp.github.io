Privacy Policy

Classic Jigsaw Puzzle  ·  Last updated: 24 November 2026

Classic Jigsaw Puzzle ("the app", "we", "us") is a jigsaw puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: we do not collect, store, share or sell your personal information.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The app does not ask you to create an account or sign in, and it does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or contacts. The app does not contain analytics tools, advertising or tracking.

2. Information stored on your device

To make the game work, the app saves some information only on your own device:

  • which puzzles you have solved or skipped;
  • your puzzle in progress, so you can continue it later;
  • your settings and preferences, such as hint usage and the screen direction of the board.

This information never leaves your device. We cannot see it. If you clear the app's data or uninstall the app, it is deleted.

3. Your own photos

The app lets you turn a photo from your gallery into a puzzle. When you choose a photo, it is used only inside the app on your device, where it is resized and saved so you can continue the puzzle later. The photo is never uploaded, sent to us or shared with anyone. Choosing "Quit" on a puzzle made from your own photo, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app deletes it. The app does not need access to your camera and does not read your other photos.

4. Internet access and third parties

The game works without an internet connection. The app does not send any information about you to us or to third parties. The app contains a link to our other game on Google Play. If you open it, you leave this app and Google's own terms and privacy policy apply to that page.

5. Advertising

This version of the app does not show advertisements. If we add advertising in the future, we will update this policy before the change is released and describe what is collected and by whom.

6. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. It contains no chat, social features, purchases or links that let a player contact other people.

7. Security

Because your information stays on your device, its safety depends on the security of your device. We recommend keeping your device's operating system up to date and protected with a screen lock.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last changed. If we make a significant change, we will say so in the app's update notes.
